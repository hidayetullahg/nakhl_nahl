# NAKHL&NAHL Getting Started Guide (Hızlı Başlangıç)

## 1. Quick Start Checklist
Welcome to NAKHL&NAHL ERP! Follow these quick steps to launch your tenant:

1. **Log in:** Access your organization via email/password or SSO.
2. **Launch Setup Wizard:** Click `❓ Yardım` → `Kurulum Sihirbazı` or follow the automated Welcome Dialog.
3. **Configure Company & Branch:** Verify tax IDs, base currency, and default branch.
4. **Create Warehouses:** Define at least one active warehouse (e.g. `Main Warehouse`).
5. **Import or Add Products:** Populate products and initial opening stocks.
6. **Set up E-Invoice:** Go to `Ayarlar` → `Entegrasyonlar`, pick Turkey (GİB) or Saudi Arabia (ZATCA), test connectivity in Sandbox.
7. **Issue your First Invoice:** Navigate to `Satış` → `Yeni Fatura` and issue your first invoice!
